# Development Vision

GeneCoder aims to provide an end-to-end simulation and research platform for DNA data storage. Key goals:

- **Integrated pipeline** covering synthesis, storage degradation, sequencing, encoding and error correction.
- **Flexible experimentation** to compare encoders, decoders and biophysical constraints without costly wet-lab trials.
- **Balance physical and logical redundancy** through rich modeling of error sources and ECC strategies.
- **Extensible design** allowing new algorithms and analysis tools to plug into the framework.
